BYBIT SPOT SCANNER - HUONG DAN CHO TRADER CHUYEN NGHIEP

Muc dich
--------
Scanner nay la cong cu ho tro ra quyet dinh theo thoi gian thuc cho trader spot.

No khong phai bot mua/ban.
No khong phai he thong du doan.
No la bo quet chuyen dong som, giup phat hien coin co hanh vi thi truong thay doi truoc khi bien dong gia tro nen qua ro rang.

Cau hoi chinh:

"Nhung coin nao dang xung dang duoc mo chart kiem tra ngay bay gio?"


Logic cot loi
-------------
He thong lang nghe du lieu live tu Bybit Spot.

Voi moi symbol, he thong phan tich:
- gia live;
- lenh khop;
- volume ngan han;
- do sau bid/ask;
- boi canh orderbook;
- lich su gia va volume;
- suc manh tuong doi so voi BTC.

Luon xu ly noi bo:

du lieu thi truong
-> realtime metrics
-> tinh score
-> raw signal
-> market context
-> phan loai cau truc va rui ro
-> alert card
-> visual chart analysis neu can


Score co nghia la gi
--------------------
Score the hien muc do bat thuong cua hoat dong hien tai so voi hanh vi gan day cua coin.

Score khong phai tin hieu vao lenh truc tiep.
No co nghia la: coin nay can duoc kiem tra.

Score bi anh huong boi:
- volume bat thuong;
- relative strength so voi BTC;
- squeeze hoac compression;
- dau hieu absorption;
- hanh vi gia ngan han;
- boi canh bid/ask.

Cach hieu chuyen nghiep:

Score thap:
Thuong khong can hanh dong.

Score trung binh:
Co hoat dong. Dua vao watchlist.

Score cao:
Can mo chart kiem tra ngay.

Score rat cao:
Co the dang vao pha momentum, nhung trap risk cung cao hon.


Alert Card
----------
Score
Do manh cua tin hieu hien tai.

Tier
Chat luong setup:
- A-TIER: cau truc manh;
- B-TIER: chap nhan duoc nhung chua hoan hao;
- C-TIER: yeu, nhieu nhieu, hoac rui ro.

Status
Vong doi tin hieu:
- RAW: tin hieu som, chua xac nhan;
- CONFIRMED: chuyen dong duoc xac nhan;
- FAILED: khong co tiep dien;
- TRAP: co the la fake move hoac bay momentum.

Risk
Danh gia rui ro dong:
- LOW: cau truc sach hon;
- MEDIUM: tinh huong lan lon;
- HIGH: bat on, qua keo, hoac nguy hiem.

Trap
Xac suat day la chuyen dong momentum gia.

Context
Dien giai thi truong va cau truc:
- trang thai thi truong;
- relative strength;
- chat luong breakout;
- trang thai structure;
- ly do he thong danh gia nhu vay.


Workflow chuyen nghiep
----------------------
Khong vao lenh chi vi co alert.

Alert la bo loc.
Chart moi la noi ra quyet dinh.

Quy trinh de xuat:

1. Xem high-score signals truoc.
2. Mo TradingView.
3. Kiem tra 5m, 15m va 1h.
4. Danh dau local range.
5. Xac dinh support va resistance.
6. Kiem tra volume co tiep dien khong.
7. Tranh mua vao nen tang dung qua manh.
8. Dung visual-check cho tin hieu manh hoac chua ro.
9. Chi can nhac giao dich khi structure xac nhan y tuong.


5m / 15m / 1h
-------------
5m
Cho thay trigger gan nhat.

15m
Cho thay chuyen dong co tiep dien that khong.

1h
Cho thay boi canh lon hon. Neu gia o ngay duoi resistance 1h, tin hieu 5m van co the rui ro.


Setup spot tot
--------------
Mot long setup tot thuong co:

- score cao;
- A-TIER hoac B-TIER manh;
- LOW hoac MEDIUM risk;
- trap probability thap;
- breakout hoac reclaim mot level quan trong;
- volume tiep dien sau impulse dau tien;
- 15m xac nhan;
- khong co resistance 1h qua gan;
- visual-check = CONFIRMED hoac WAIT voi trigger ro rang.


Setup yeu
---------
Nen ha uu tien hoac bo qua khi:

- gia da pump thang dung;
- co bong nen tren dai;
- volume spike nhung khong tiep dien;
- gia bi reject tai resistance;
- 15m khong xac nhan;
- 1h bearish hoac ngay duoi resistance;
- trap cao;
- visual-check = REJECTED.


Visual Check
------------
Visual-check la buoc kiem tra chart thu cong voi GPT.

No phan tich:
- 5m;
- 15m;
- 1h.

Ket qua gom:
- visual status;
- entry quality;
- risk;
- trend theo tung timeframe;
- summary;
- reasons;
- invalidation;
- next action.

CONFIRMED
Cau truc chart ung ho tin hieu scanner.

WAIT
Tin hieu dang quan tam nhung can doi xac nhan.

REJECTED
Cau truc khong ung ho mot entry chat luong.


Quy tac ra quyet dinh
---------------------
Tin hieu tot nhat khong phai tin hieu co score cao nhat.

Tin hieu tot nhat la noi:

scanner activity
+ market context
+ price structure
+ volume continuation
+ risk control

cung chi ve mot huong.


Nguyen tac cuoi cung
--------------------
Scanner tim diem can chu y.
Chart xac nhan structure.
Trader kiem soat rui ro.

Alert khong phai menh lenh.
Alert la trigger cho watchlist chuyen nghiep.
