BYBIT SPOT SCANNER - PROFESSIONAL TRADER GUIDE

Purpose
-------
This scanner is a real-time decision support tool for spot traders.

It is not a buy/sell bot.
It is not a prediction system.
It is an early movement scanner that helps a trader quickly find coins where market behavior changes before the move becomes obvious.

The scanner is designed to answer one practical question:

"Which coins deserve immediate chart review right now?"


Core Data Flow
--------------
The system listens to live Bybit Spot market streams.

For every active symbol it receives:
- live price updates;
- trade prints;
- short-term volume;
- bid/ask depth;
- orderbook imbalance;
- historical price and volume lists;
- relative behavior versus BTC.

The internal flow is:

market data
-> realtime metrics
-> score calculation
-> raw signal
-> market context
-> structure/risk classification
-> alert card
-> optional visual chart analysis


What The Score Means
--------------------
Score is the scanner's estimate of abnormal short-term activity.

It is not a direct entry signal.
It means that the coin is behaving differently from its recent baseline.

Score is influenced by:
- volume anomaly;
- relative strength versus BTC;
- squeeze or compression;
- absorption signs;
- short-term price behavior;
- bid/ask depth context.

Professional interpretation:

Low score:
Market is close to normal behavior. Usually no action.

Medium score:
There is activity. Put the coin on watch and check structure.

High score:
The coin deserves immediate chart review.

Very high score:
The coin may be entering an active momentum phase, but this also increases trap risk.


Signal Card Fields
------------------
Score
Current strength of the scanner signal.

Tier
Setup quality classification:
- A-TIER: strong structure and better continuation profile;
- B-TIER: acceptable but imperfect setup;
- C-TIER: weak, noisy, or low-quality setup.

Status
Signal lifecycle:
- RAW: early signal, not confirmed yet;
- CONFIRMED: post-signal behavior supports continuation;
- FAILED: continuation did not appear;
- TRAP: rejection or fake momentum behavior detected.

Risk
Dynamic risk estimate:
- LOW: cleaner structure, lower trap signs;
- MEDIUM: mixed or volatile structure;
- HIGH: unstable price action, exhaustion, or trap probability.

Trap
Probability that the move is a false momentum event.
High trap probability means the move may already be extended or rejected.

Context
Market and structure interpretation:
- broader market state;
- relative strength;
- breakout quality;
- structure status;
- explanation of the classification.


How To Use Alerts Professionally
--------------------------------
Do not enter only because an alert appeared.

The alert is a filter.
The chart is the decision surface.

Recommended workflow:

1. Watch the high-score section first.
2. Open the TradingView chart.
3. Check 5m, 15m, and 1h.
4. Identify the local range.
5. Mark support and resistance.
6. Check whether the move has continuation volume.
7. Check whether price is already extended.
8. Use visual-check for strong or unclear signals.
9. Decide only after structure confirms the idea.


5m / 15m / 1h Interpretation
----------------------------
5m
Shows the immediate trigger.
Useful for impulse, rejection, retest, and entry timing.

15m
Shows whether the move has real continuation.
This timeframe is usually more important than 5m for avoiding noise.

1h
Shows broader context.
If price is directly under 1h resistance, a strong 5m signal may still be risky.


Good Spot Setup
---------------
A better long setup usually has:

- high score;
- A-TIER or strong B-TIER;
- LOW or MEDIUM risk;
- low trap probability;
- price breaking or reclaiming an important level;
- volume continuing after the first impulse;
- 15m confirming the move;
- 1h not blocking price with nearby resistance;
- visual-check result CONFIRMED or WAIT with a clear trigger.


Weak Or Dangerous Setup
-----------------------
Avoid or downgrade the signal when:

- price already pumped vertically;
- there is a long upper wick;
- volume spike appears without continuation;
- price rejects resistance;
- 15m does not confirm;
- 1h is bearish or under strong resistance;
- trap probability is high;
- visual-check says REJECTED.


Visual Check
------------
Visual-check is a manual GPT-assisted chart review.

It analyzes screenshots from:
- 5m;
- 15m;
- 1h.

It returns:
- visual status;
- entry quality;
- risk;
- timeframe trends;
- Russian and English summary;
- reasons;
- invalidation condition;
- next action.

Visual status:

CONFIRMED
The chart structure supports the scanner signal.

WAIT
The signal is interesting, but the chart still needs confirmation.

REJECTED
The chart does not support a quality entry.


Professional Decision Rule
--------------------------
The best signal is not the highest score.

The best signal is the one where:

scanner activity
+ market context
+ price structure
+ volume continuation
+ risk control

all point in the same direction.


Practical Entry Logic For Spot
------------------------------
A practical spot trader can use this sequence:

1. Alert appears.
2. Check if score is high enough.
3. Check Tier and Risk.
4. Open TradingView.
5. Find the level that price must hold.
6. Wait for breakout, retest, or continuation volume.
7. Avoid buying into vertical candles.
8. Define invalidation before entry.
9. Use smaller position if risk is MEDIUM.
10. Skip if structure is unclear.


What To Do With WAIT
--------------------
WAIT is not negative.

It means:
- the coin is interesting;
- the scanner found activity;
- but the chart has not confirmed a clean entry yet.

Common WAIT conditions:
- wait for retest;
- wait for volume continuation;
- wait for candle close above resistance;
- wait for 15m confirmation;
- wait for BTC/market stability.


Final Principle
---------------
The scanner finds attention.
The chart confirms structure.
The trader controls risk.

Never treat an alert as a command.
Treat it as a professional watchlist trigger.
